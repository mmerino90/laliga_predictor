How good is it?

How it works

  • Data: every La Liga match since 2015-16 from football-data.co.uk: results, shots and pre-match bookmaker odds.
  • Features (all computed from information available before kick-off): ELO ratings that carry across seasons, rolling form over the last 3/5/10 matches, shots on target, home/away-specific form, head-to-head, rest days, and the bookmakers' implied probabilities.
  • Models: an XGBoost classifier for home/draw/away and two Poisson XGBoost regressors for the goals each side scores, which give the scoreline probabilities.
  • Evaluation: walk-forward. Each season is predicted by a model trained only on the seasons before it, and every model is compared with the bookmakers on the same matches.
  • What "Bookmakers" means: betting odds are a forecast in disguise. Odds of 2.00 mean "about 50%". Converting the odds to probabilities and removing the bookmakers' built-in profit margin gives the betting market's own prediction. It is not a model trained here; it is the benchmark to beat.
  • The honest result: the model is close to the market but does not beat it. Betting markets already price in everything these features capture.
