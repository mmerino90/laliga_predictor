How it works

01 · DATA

Every match since 2015

Results, shots and pre-match odds for 4,000+ La Liga matches from football-data.co.uk, refreshed every morning.

02 · FEATURES

Only what's known before kick-off

ELO ratings, form over 3/5/10 games, shots on target, home/away form, head-to-head, rest days and market odds.

03 · MODELS

XGBoost, twice

A classifier for home/draw/away, and two Poisson regressors for each side's goals, which give every scoreline a probability.

04 · EVALUATION

Walk-forward, no peeking

Each season is predicted by a model trained only on earlier seasons, then compared with the bookmakers on the same matches.

%

What "bookmakers" means. Betting odds are a forecast in disguise: odds of 2.00 mean "about 50%". The site converts pre-match odds from Bet365 and the market average into probabilities and removes the bookmakers' profit margin, e.g. odds 2.00 / 3.40 / 3.80 → 47% / 28% / 25%. That is the market's own prediction, not a model trained here, and the hardest benchmark there is. The honest result: the model gets close but does not beat it.
